      How we check a deal

      1. Read the price at the store. Every deal here comes from the store's own Black Friday release or page, or from a deals source that names the store — never from a guess.
      2. Show the “was” price honestly. Where a store only states the saving, the “was” price is the sale price plus that saving. Where a store published no usual price, the deal is left out.
      3. Call it like it is. New low means the lowest price the source has ever recorded. Strong deal means 40% off or more on a current model. Good deal is a solid 20–39% cut. Routine is a modest 10–19%. Modest is under 10% but still the first cut on the item. Skip means barely a discount at all — we list it so you don't buy it by mistake.
      4. Date every check. Each card says when its price was read and where. Amazon prices move daily: ones reported 20–23 Nov are marked “not re-checked”.

      Prices can change during the day. Always confirm the price at checkout before you pay. DealSleuth is an independent finder and is not affiliated with Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, Costco or any brand listed.
